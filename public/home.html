About Greens for Growth

Rooted in Growth.

Greens for Growth Farming School is an accredited organic training farm located in Indang, Cavite. We are dedicated to building a sustainable future by empowering communities through practical ecological practices.

Whether you are an aspiring agripreneur or a conscious consumer, our mission is to make sustainable farming accessible, educational, and deeply rewarding.

Education & Training

Empowering Agripreneurs.

Through strategic partnerships with TESDA and the Department of Agriculture, we offer certified programs designed to equip students with top-tier sustainable farming techniques and agribusiness management skills.

Our curriculum bridges traditional land stewardship with modern agricultural standards, guiding learners from seed to market success.

Platform & Produce

Connected Convenience.

Beyond training, our facility functions as an active organic farm supplying fresh, chemical-free produce directly to our local community.

Through GreenLink, you can seamlessly explore available farm harvest items, book school appointments, and submit direct inquiries all in one unified digital space.
